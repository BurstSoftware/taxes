# Some general tax concepts to keep in mind:

Tax brackets: The United States has a progressive tax system, with different tax rates applying to different levels of income. The tax brackets for the 2022 tax year are as follows:
10% on the first $10,275 of taxable income
12% on taxable income between 10,276 and 41,775
22% on taxable income between 41,776 and 89,075
24% on taxable income between 89,076 and 170,050
32% on taxable income between 170,051 and 215,950
35% on taxable income between 215,951 and 539,900
37% on taxable income over $539,900
Tax deductions: These are expenses you can subtract from your income to reduce your taxable income. Common tax deductions include mortgage interest, charitable donations, and medical expenses.
Tax credits: These are direct reductions to your tax liability. Common tax credits include the Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC) and the Child Tax Credit.
Please keep in mind that tax laws and regulations are subject to change, and this information may not be up-to-date.
